# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The canonical text — including expected behavior, unacceptable behavior, enforcement guidelines, and the scope of the policy — lives at the link above and applies in full to every Frick space (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any direct communication about the project).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation, please report it to the maintainer at `bri@briannadoubt.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge the report within 3 business days and follow the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) when deciding how to respond.

For security vulnerabilities (as opposed to conduct issues), see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) — that channel is separate and is the right one for issues that affect users of the framework.
